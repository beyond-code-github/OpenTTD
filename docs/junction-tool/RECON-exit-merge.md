## Throughput guidance found (all of it)

- ABR10: "We also assign the priority role to the tunnels." (merger priority sits on the tunnel lines.) "it is wise to cross the lines later on" (about saving space atop the tunnels).
- ABR06: "Out of the Hub: ... you just swap the tracks when in safe position. This technique is very friendly for usage." and "Solve Inside ... saves you space behind the hub, while sacrificing some inside."
- ABR06 bridge text: "highly friendly with presignals which are just best unless you want to prio the bridges."
- Nowhere do the articles say "merge before crossing" or "keep lanes separate as long as possible". Grep for those phrasings found nothing.
- Tunnels/bridges: tunnels dominant, triples likely ("triple tunnels are very likely"), 4 tunnels at TL2. Bridges are short, doubled decks (see RECON-hub-bridges.md). Bays/signals between merge and crossing: signals visible on open track between portal groups, bays not identifiable.

## Conclusion

Seen: in the ABR10 invisible-hub style, lanes are split out, run as separate parallel tunnels (2-4 mouths) under the crossing, and the merger is placed at the tunnel end, after the crossing; this is the only structural evidence and it is the shape of (b). No screenshot clearly shows a single merged exit track crossing the other direction (a), but the images are mostly too small or too dense to exclude it. Inferred (not stated by any source): in coop hubs generally the crossing is done by several lanes in parallel tunnels because tunnels are cheap in tiles and the merge, with its priority, is built where there is open space "outside the hub". The sources give no throughput rule on merge order, so the choice between (a) and (b) for the generator cannot be taken from ABR; it needs the wiki hub pages or the checker.
