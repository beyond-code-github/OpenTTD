## 1. What was built
- The brief's model: one JunctionPositions variable per track tile (range ground-3..ground+3 over all tiles), difference constraints per route: consecutive tiles `|L_b - L_a| <= 1` if a is a ramp-capable tile, else `==`; ramp rule `L(t+tl) - L(t) <= k` per route (not across a bridge middle); crosser `<=` both heads; ports, nodes, heads flat (not ramp-capable) but variables. No "no levels" can remain except by the range (heights never fail: confirmed, the 8 + 8 "no levels" of Z2c became 0).
- Level of a tile = its ENTRY level in travel direction (needed so a ramp tile's exit is the next tile's variable). Ramp-capable = lone axis track on every passage, all passages the same direction (shared runs and doubled copies may ramp together; INFERRED not measured: how many tiles carry opposite directions and are thereby forced flat), not node/port/head/route end.
- Start: tiles taken in order heads, nodes/ports, rest; each at its cheapest flat level within its propagated [Lo, Up], then RestrictRange (trail undone before the descent). Descent: groups tied by `==` (union-find), per group restrict only its constraint neighbours to their current levels (exact: others fixed), read Lo/Up, Undo, try every level, cost = Z2c's Evaluate table for the group and its predecessor ramp tiles plus corner mismatch `terraform_cost x |delta|` against every adjacent track tile. Stop on no improvement (rounds: mean 2.2, max 5, limit 20). Plan stage as Z2c (fixed corners, propagate).
- A bug worth knowing: leaving the initial RestrictRange calls on the trail freezes every variable (Lo == Up), the descent then never moves.

## 3. What fails and why (groups; the brief model alone)
1. Brief model alone: 110 of 117 ridge failures and all 16 fixture failures are `terraform conflict`: two adjacent track tiles demand different heights at a shared corner. Two causes:
   a. FIXTURES, 9 of 17 conflicts: a tile on a foundation demands its exact ground corners, so its neighbour on the same edge (flat at the same level, one corner raised) "conflicts" with it. Not a real conflict: a foundation tile takes any corner between its ground and its level. Fix = FLEX (above).
   b. All other conflicts (ridges all, fixtures 6-8): parallel lanes one tile apart ramp at different tiles (row 5's lane has its only ramp-capable tile at a turn, row 4's lane ramps one tile off; the cost penalty cannot move it because the second ramp 4 tiles on is pinned by the ramp rule: a ramp slide is a two-variable move). Fix = ADJ: the shared corner of two track tiles is one height, written on the entry/exit variables (a corner on the exit side of a ramp tile is the NEXT tile's variable); it is a plain difference constraint, the descent then needs no moves to fix it.
2. After ADJ + FLEX: 2 fixtures and ridges with a corner of a track tile "fixed at h, would move to h+-1": the terraform slope limit between track corners 2 tiles apart (a gap tile between two track tiles whose corner heights differ by 2). Fix = SLOPE2 (a difference constraint). Remaining after that: temperate-mountainous-5 (corner (156, 31)) and ridge col 18 h 4 (corner (20, 7) of tile (20, 7)): a corner of a ground-following or foundation tile (its height is the GROUND's, not its level variable) pulled by a neighbouring terraform. INFERRED cause. PROPOSED: for a tile that follows the ground, the corner heights are constants known per level choice: add its distance-2 neighbours as unary range limits on the neighbour's level once its level is chosen (a descent cost: delta beyond d x terraform_cost = infinite) rather than difference constraints.
3. Margin artefact: at margin 1, 35 ridges fail "touches tile outside the snapshot" (a cut across a ridge as wide as the box spills out of the one-tile margin); with margin 8 these solve. Real snapshots have terrain beyond: the plan should treat outside-snapshot corners as free to move at the terraform price, or the snapshot margin should be >= the largest cut (ridge h) tiles. PROPOSED.
4. Not solved by this model: nothing remains of "no levels" (Z2c: 8 of 18 fixtures, 52 ridges). Corners spilling into Water/Blocked/Rail tiles (the corner-touch failures when levels are degenerate, see 4.1) are hard facts that appear only through the propagation: PROPOSED a descent penalty for any moved corner whose four tiles are not all Buildable (tried for the corners the tile demands directly, no effect, as the spill is second order; needs the propagated plan).

## 4. Findings outside the model
1. The fixture JSONs carry no `terraform_cost` (JunctionTerrain default 0): every real-terrain level solve in the committed sweep is run with free terraform, so levels degenerate to the lowest tie (-3 for all tiles) and the cost table is meaningless. Z2c's 1 of 18 is a tc=0 result (0 of 18 at tc=100). At tc=0 even the best config solves only 8 of 18 (spill into Water/Blocked). PROPOSED: the fixtures carry the game's price (the dump tool), or the sweep sets 100 as the ridge test does.
2. In 4 fixtures (arctic-hilly-13, arctic-hilly-9, temperate-hilly-4, temperate-mountainous-4) the placement in terraform mode (columns, width 41) differs from the hard-facts placement (width 50, 52, 43, 42). The committed sweep's `again == hard` check would fail for them regardless of levels. Not investigated (INFERRED: the terraform-mode call skips a hard fact the hard mode applies, or vice versa).
3. T3 findings (tile, edge, why): none; no solved case failed JunctionRoutesHeightsOk on the terraformed ground.

## 5. Proposed next step
Make the joint model the solver: per-tile entry-level variables, ramp/flat continuity, ramp rule, bridge crossers, ADJ shared-corner equalities, SLOPE2 spill limits, FLEX in the plan merge, greedy start + group descent (2-5 rounds). Then: (a) unary ground-following corner limits (3.2), (b) outside-snapshot corners free (3.3), (c) fixtures with a terraform price (4.1), (d) pair moves or an exact solve for the cost (the descent is 1-2% above Z2c on 10 of 107 ridges), (e) tiles with opposite-direction axis tracks: measure how many are forced flat. Hover: 7 ms mean, 13 ms max (SLOPE2 is most of it; fewer pairs by indexing on corners would cut it), inside 100 ms.
