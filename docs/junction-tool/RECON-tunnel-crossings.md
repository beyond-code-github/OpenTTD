## 1. The game's tunnel rules (src/tunnelbridge_cmd.cpp CmdBuildTunnel :641-800)
- Entry portal tile: its slope must be one of the four inclined slopes (GetInclinedSlopeDirection :667-669); the tunnel runs in the direction the slope rises. No foundation is taken into account (the raw slope), so a portal needs the terraform, never a foundation (unlike a bridge head, junction_levels.cpp GetBridgeHeadGround).
- Both portals at one height: the loop (:700-723) walks on from the entry and stops at the FIRST tile whose lowest corner z equals the entry's z (`start_z == end_z`, :709). That tile is the exit portal, whatever lies between. A tile in the middle at the floor's own z therefore ENDS the tunnel early.
- Exit portal slope: must be ComplementSlope of the entry's (:748); if it is not, the game terraforms it to `end_tileh & start_tileh` (:749-772) and charges that (failing as "unable to excavate").
- Ground between: not checked beyond "z_min != start_z". It may be higher (the normal case) or lower (the tunnel then floats in air; the game allows it). Houses, rail, road and industries on the tiles above are not checked at all: a tunnel passes under anything (the clearing is done only on the two portal tiles, :655 and :738). INFERRED for new building ON TOP of a tunnel afterwards (CheckBuildAbove_TunnelBridge :2146, not read).
- Another tunnel in the way: a middle tile where a tunnel of the other axis runs at the same floor z refuses (IsTunnelInWayDir :711, tunnel_map.cpp:48); different depth is fine (this is D155's "tunnels cross only at different depths"). Terraforming may not lower a tile above a tunnel (terraform_cmd.cpp:250), so a plan's raises are safe, its lowerings are not.
- Max length: `tiles > max_tunnel_length` refuses (:732); the setting is 64 by default (table/settings/world_settings.ini:510-518). JunctionSpec already has max_tunnel_length (junction_generator.h:53).
- Price: per middle tile BuildTunnel with a cost growth `cost += cost >> coef` (coef 3, +1 at 25 and 50...) (:721-722), plus both portals, plus rail (tiles+2)*RailBuildCost, plus the clearing of the portals and the exit's terraform. JunctionTunnelPrice (junction_game.cpp:694) mirrors it without the clearing; spec.prices.tunnel(n) exists.
- Train z: a ground vehicle in a tunnel entrance is at the tile's z (GetSlopePixelZ_TunnelBridge :1726): no climb on a portal (a flat bridge head climbs, :1733). So the tunnel track is flat at the floor level across portal and middle.

## 2. Per stage (what a crossing as tunnel changes)
Geometry in levels: the under track runs flat at floor z; the portal tiles are inclined from z (outer edge) to z+1 (tunnel side); every middle tile has z_min >= z+1; any crosser tile on the middle must be at level >= z+1 (a crosser flat at z would end the tunnel). Compare a bridge: crosser <= heads' z, a span-height cap. A tunnel on flat ground needs the middle ground RAISED by one level (terraform); a bridge needs nothing. So tunnels win on rising ground and when stacks grow tall (LLL_RRR), bridges on flat ground.
1. Structure (StructureJunctionTrackGraph, junction_topology.cpp:677, bridges :748-762): JunctionTrackBridge gains `bool tunnel`. For a tunnel the CARRIER (edge) is the UNDER edge and `crossed` are the edges that pass over it: so which edge is the carrier flips from today's rule (branch over main, main row edge over its crossers) to D223/D190 (turning leg under; main line under for a main tunnel). A doubled main lane: both copies tunnel (two parallel tunnels, no same-depth crossing problem).
2. Programs (ProgramJunctionTrackGraph :785-1000; tokens junction_topology.h:206-212): carrier gets a Tunnel token per crossed edge in place of Over; the crossed edges get an Above token in place of Under (the tiles over the middle; no S-bend before it, since the S-bend before Under exists to land the under track in the span). New Kind values Tunnel and Above, or a flag on Over/Under (plainer: flag, 2 enum uses stay).
3. Placement (PlaceTrackGraph :1073; Heads elem :1255, relations :2469, hug :2177/:2389, clearance :2227/:2542): portals are the same Heads element (two tiles on the carrier's row/column, run before them >= 1 axis tile since 'T' needs an axis piece, junction_route.cpp:107-112, so the D110 "bridge completes the bend" case is not available). Hug and clearance relations apply unchanged in a first cut; the length cap becomes max_tunnel_length (64) in place of max_bridge_middle (:3258). No span-height relation exists in placement (it is a level matter), so none to drop.

Needed: (a) portal Visit with dir = into the tunnel and a head option "rise" (outer corners at L, tunnel-side corners at L+1; EvaluateHead :105 kind 2 mirrored; no foundation option); (b) both portals at one level (existing `diff` pair :518-521); (c) every middle tile's corners >= L+1 (the span bound t_span_lo/hi :674-720 turned from an upper to a lower bound); crossers on the middle at level >= L+1 (reverse of :526-531); (d) ramp rule: portal and middle are flat for the train, a window across the tunnel counts 0 rise (simpler than IG4's bridge profile).

## 3. Minimal first cut
Plainest model: a per-bridge flag `tunnel` fixed by a rule, no cost search. Candidate rule R_A: tunnel iff the bridge's span ground (the solved ground of the middle) would lie above the heads' level, i.e. the IG3 span-height check fails for the bridge at every head level reachable; needs the level solve to run before the choice, so it is a retry loop (bridge first; on an IG3 failure naming a bridge, flip that bridge to a tunnel and re-run) and the structure stage cannot decide it. Candidate rule R_B: always tunnel for a chosen class (e.g. the branch joiner crossing the main line, D190 "main line over or under" gives the cheapest compact one). R_B needs no solve but forces terraform raises on flat ground, so it is a regression where bridges were free. Recommendation: the retry flip (R_A) with the structure's bridge list taking `tunnel=false` by default, because it changes nothing where bridges work (identity test) and serves D220's stated case (wide hubs on rising ground). The flip is per bridge (granularity: carrier + its crossed set). A true cost choice in the level solve (both options in the solver) is not minimal: the structure, programs and placement differ per option, so it is a search over structures (D189), not a solve variable.
Needs from the engineer: (1) confirm the flip rule (R_A over R_B), (2) tunnel carrier flips to the under edge (D223): for a tunnel under a joiner crossing, which edge dives; accept the D223/D190 rule as given, (3) whether the doubled main lane may be in a tunnel (two parallel tunnels, each a full price) or stays bridge-only for the first cut (recommended: bridge-only doubled lane first, joiner tunnels first; since a tunnel under a prio-pair is the dominant coop pattern the doubled case is step 6).
