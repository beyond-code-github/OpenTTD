## 1. Units

- A unit is half a tile of axis travel. Axis piece (X or Y track, `IsDiagonalTrackdir`, track_func.h:526) = 2 units; a half piece (UPPER/LOWER/LEFT/RIGHT) = 1 unit; every bridge tile (heads and middle) = 2 units (junction_route.cpp:70; junction_station_generator.cpp:31). Router state `u` adds 2 per axis piece, 1 per half piece (junction_line_router.cpp:~318 `AdvanceState`; junction_router.cpp:349).
- A half piece occupies one whole tile. A diagonal run of d half pieces is a staircase of d tiles, d/2 columns and d/2 rows. A one-row S-bend `RFL` = 2 half pieces in ONE x column on two rows, flanked by axis pieces (RECON-lane-geometry.md "c. Derived").
- Gap between two curves = units from the first turn piece's start to the second turn piece's start (the first turn piece counts). Rule 3 measures the same thing: `CurveSpeedLimitOfTrain` walks parts (one per unit, vehicle_length 1), a curve is a direction change between two consecutive parts, `sum/numcurve` = mean gap in units (junction_rules.cpp:540-585). JunctionMinCurveSpeed pads the path ends with n straight parts and takes the least limit over every n-part window (junction_rules.cpp:642-656).
- Speed limit by mean gap g (units), curve_speed 0: 232-(13-clamp(g,1,12))^2: g=1 -> 88 (any two curves <= 1 unit apart also forces 88), 3 -> 132, 4 -> 151, 5 -> 168. Exactly one left and one right curve in a window is exempt (limit unlimited).
- Move strings (router trace) are NOT tile counts. A turn move L/R is tried with 0, 1, 2 extra straight half pieces so the turn lands on a piece that can turn (`ExtendJunctionLine`, junction_line.cpp:208-240, "TurnStrings"); parity: a same-hand 90-degree corner needs an ODD number of half pieces between its two turns, an S-bend an EVEN number. So `LFFFL` is 5 halves (the last L is `FL`), `RFFFFL` 6. Tiles in section 5 are decoded with this and cross-checked against flat-T numbers in DESIGN-nodes §5 and RECON-lane-geometry.
- Signal spacing weight: axis 192, half 128; violation when d > sd*192 = 384 at a legal piece (junction_rules_internal.h:21; rules.cpp:812-822). So at most 2 axis tiles (or 3 half pieces) without a signal when a legal piece exists.
- Prio coverage and bays are in units too: coverage 2*prio_tiles = 28 units (rules.cpp G9c ~1300), bay = 2*tl = 8 units (rules.cpp:~878).

## 2. Edge types

"Before"/"after" = straight units between the previous/next curve point and this edge's first/last turn. Two bounds: ROUTER = in-edge rule `TurnAllowed` (junction_line_router.cpp:~268-285, same in junction_router.cpp:308-318); RULE3 = checker rule 3, which CS3 (briefs/CS3-boundary-by-rule3.md, junction_generator.cpp:1050-1058) uses across edge boundaries (approach + route + continuation, `BoundaryOk` junction_line_router.cpp:408-421) while the router's own rule still governs inside one edge.

ROUTER rule, exact: a turn is legal if (a) u >= CL (5), or (b) it closes a tight S-bend: previous turn of the OTHER hand and the gap before that previous turn was >= SW (8) (`prev_gap_ok`, set from u at each turn); after a tight pair the next turn needs u >= SW (8). Otherwise refused.
RULE3, exact for window n=8 (spans <= 6 units): three curves cannot share a window unless mean gap >= 5, impossible, so any third curve must be >= 7 units from the first of the previous two. Two same-hand curves alone need gap >= 5; two opposite-hand curves alone (1L+1R) are exempt but gap <= 1 gives 88. General formula INFERRED from the code: gap to the curve beyond an S-bend >= n-1-2 = 2*tl-3 units; same-hand pair >= CL.

## 4. Signal slots

- Slot = a piece for which `IsLegalSignalPiece` holds (rules.cpp:~700): non-bridge, not a bridge head, its tile has no overlapping tracks (a half piece alone on a tile counts), no signal already on its track. A switch tile is never a slot (isolation, D117a).
- Straight edge: slot on every legal piece; max gap 2 axis tiles (sd 2) between signals (G6 4).
- Corner / S-bend half pieces: legal only when alone on the tile. Lane geometry: lane B's second half piece at the lane split and lane A's first half piece at the lane merge are the single legal pieces on those legs (RECON-lane-geometry §e).
- Before a split: one signal facing along the path in the stretch back to the previous switch or bridge, if any legal piece exists there. After a merge: same forward. Between a split and a bridge head (D113), and between a far head and a merge: at least one legal piece, else "split in a bridge's block" violation (G6, :803-810).
- Bridge: no signal on head or middle; a signal before the near head and after the far head: the bridge is its own block (D80, D82).
- Prio: signals on the prio row face upstream, one between consecutive segments, Exit farthest from the merge, Combo elsewhere (G9 5); the joiner's nearest signal before an ML merge is Entry (G9 6); lane merges have no prio.
- G8: no block holds both a split and a merge (blocks computed by ComputeBlocks, rules.cpp:~960).
- G7 bays: signal at each bay boundary on queue merges (D88).
- Block boundaries per edge are therefore: signal slot before every split, after every merge, both ends of every bridge, bay boundaries on branch edges, joins on prio rows.
