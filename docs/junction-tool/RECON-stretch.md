## 3. Classes and proposals
**C1 OCCUPANCY (X): nested in-fans.** Cause (MEASURED, LL_RR test, e0/e1): e0's fan goes from port row 4 to stack row 1 (m 3), e1's from row 5 to row 3 (m 2), both starting at x 2. e0 occupies row 3 at x 2-3; e1 reaches row 3 at x 4. Stretching e0's port-to-fan run by 1 moves e0's diagonal to e1's (3,3) td 11 and (3,4) td 10: the same pieces, so the lanes merge (rule 1). The least solution has them aligned because both start at the port column, but nothing orders them. On the E side the same: e6's fan (row 7 to 8, x 46) must come after e5's fan has left row 8 (x 49); a longer d_min moved it to x 51 and the two share (51,8).
Relation (PROPOSED then CHECKED): for two in-fans A, B on one side with fans (m > 0) and B's stack row within A's span [port row of A, stack row of A] (and A's stack row not within B's span): `along(heading, B.fil, A.fif, |sr_B - pr_A|)`, i.e. B reaches its stack row no earlier than the column where A leaves that row (A crosses row rho at fif_A + |rho - pr_A|); reason "in fan order" (edge of B, row of B). It is a lower bound in the travel sense, so stretching A pushes B.
CHECKED (scratch bit 1): the seeds are SamePlacement-identical to the unmodified seeds in all 6 cells (box 41x14, 28x13, 47x15; 62x25, 44x24, 72x30), all 11 X violating runs are gone (X rule violations 11 to 0), no new violation in 1227 runs (15 new runs from the 5 new relations are clean), the X solve errors stay 348. All 51 `[topology]` and the 2 `[topologysweep]` tests pass with the relation on (jt-equivalent filter, Release). Two stretched runs now share a tile between e5 and e6 with the checker clean (opposite half pieces, as at the seed pair e0/e1); the share detector is not a violation by itself. Not covered: in-fans against out-fans and fans of the other heading (C2).

**C2 OCCUPANCY (Y).** Cause (INFERRED, not isolated): a Y stretch moves the stack rows under the fixed port offset, so the E-side in-fans e5, e6 get larger m (fan_m is derived from the rows) and their diagonals now cross the runs of e8, e16, e24 (rows of the other heading, ending at the E ports) at columns where those rows are occupied: 12 share/touch pairs in the runs of stack rows idx 0 s 5 and chain run e15 s 5; the pieces then merge (rule 1 and its cascade). C1's relation only orders fans of one heading against each other; the missing relation is the same family: a fan's diagonal against every row it crosses and against the out-fan of an opposite-heading row at that end (an order relation per row and per column, DESIGN-G1 §14.3). UNEXPLAINED in detail: which pair is first to collide; not designed, not checked. 8 runs (3 + 3 + 2 over the cells).

## 4. Bridges
- Does `choices.hug` stay the outermost crosser tile pair? In X stretches: yes whenever the solve succeeds (531 of 531 solved X runs keep the heads' gaps to the outermost crossers: heads [lo,hi] vs crossers; no span changes); the rest are solve errors because the picks are equalities (west = low pick - 1, east = high pick + 1). Stretching a relation that moves one pick but not the other cannot be absorbed: 348 of 879 X runs (all bridge groups). So the group is already rigid in X; a stretch is never absorbed, never illegal.
- In Y stretches: no. 54 of the 264 solved Y runs lose the hug (gap to the outermost crosser grows by 1-3; no run has a crosser outside the heads, gap < 1; no span changes, so none exceeds spec.max_bridge_middle; 24 of the 54 are checker-clean, so a head that does not hug is legal, just not minimal). ## 5. Conclusions (what to design in T1b)
1. Curve-gap pairs: no X gap. X stretches never fail a curve gap alone; the only 6 failures are after a Y stretch at tl6 v196 (C4) and are a missing (lane-merge c0, out-fan c2) pair.

4. Remaining after C1 + C3: 16 Y runs (8 occupancy, 3 prio, 6 curve gap, overlap 1), all after row gap stretches, none in X.
