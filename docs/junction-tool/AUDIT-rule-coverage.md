# AUDIT-rule-coverage: which checker rules the node template guarantees (RC0, D193)

Read-only audit, 2026-11-11. Sources: DESIGN-N3.md §1-§5, src/junction_rules.{h,cpp}, src/junction_signal_pass.{h,cpp}, src/junction_nodes.cpp (AddPathDiffs :298, AddCrossings :392, AddBoxEdges :448, SolveJunctionNodes :568), DECISIONS.md. Everything marked INFERRED is reasoned, not read or measured.

Terms. "Template" = the constraints of DESIGN-N3 §2 (T1 free link >= 1, T2 curve gaps, T3 prio coverage, T4 crossings, T5 anchor) plus §5 (box edges, Y classes) plus §3 render (translate classes, fill free links, extend E/W end runs, normalise, PlaceJunctionSignals). "Pass" = PlaceJunctionSignals (junction_signal_pass.cpp:640): required signals, bay signals, fill, thinning, lane-block signals, prios. The pass only adds signals and prio tracks at tiles that already exist; it never makes room.

Structural fact that shapes the whole table: the template moves classes of points along X (and Y) and keeps every rigid run, so topology, port roles, switch order and piece types are preserved from the flat T. What it does not model is any relation between pieces of different paths other than the bridge crossings (T4). Everything below that fails does so through that gap or through a length that is not a lower bound.

## 3. Uncovered rules, most likely to fail first in the node search

Cases ranked: house at (xI-1, 0), unbuildable hub tile, W-end incline (all force detours), then 100-196 km/h on flat land.

1. Collision / edge contact of unrelated tracks (Connectivity unexpected/ambiguous, Geometry, Bridges head tile, track overlap): every S-bend detour (D182) moves a row next to another path; this is the first thing the oracle will report. Occupancy rule, needs design.
2. Prio row (Prio, PrioReach): the row beside the ML over the coverage is a footprint nothing reserves; a detour near a merge puts a foreign track in it, giving "missing prio" or "prio track touches a used track". Derived-footprint rule, needs design.
3. WaitingRoom (both parts: split-to-merge >= 2 TL + tail; bay >= 2 TL): not a template rule at all. Binding when links shrink: hub/house squeeze, and at 100-140 km/h once N0c2 sizes diagonals by the rule. Plain difference constraints (section 1).
4. Deadlock and Signals slots: a split and a merge, or a split and a bridge, pulled to within one piece. Difference constraint >= 2 pieces between switches (and head).
5. Order, adjacent ML splits: not binding in the T; needed once an ML carries two splits (LL_RR). Difference constraint >= 2.
6. Bridges overlap (two bridges on one line) and Footprint boundary contents: only with several bridges on one row/column or a prio row on the last row/column. Differences to the box edge.
7. Connectivity doubled-lane equality: only with doubled lanes that get unequal stretching (LL_RR).
8. PrioReach residual: bridge head or foreign switch inside a coverage. T3 extension.

Covered: CurveSpeed (T2), Signals (pass, subject to item 4).

## RC1 re-audit (2026-11-11, head 952af32ad3)

Read-only paper audit of RC0 against src/junction_nodes.cpp after N0c2 (free diagonals, two-phase solve), OC1 (AddJunctionNodeOccupancy :744, chain loop :831-878), RU1 (keep_slot :474, add_room, rules 4-6 of AddPathDiffs :330) and PR1 (prio_rows built in AddPathDiffs rule 3, entered as an element in AddJunctionNodeOccupancy ~:822-828). Checker functions at junction_rules.cpp: CheckConnectivity :347, CheckGeometry :418, CheckOrder :429, CheckBridges :470, CheckCurveSpeed :616, CheckSignals :731, CheckWaitingRoom :843, CheckDeadlock :1014, CheckPrio :1054, CheckPrioReach :1535, CheckFootprint :1632 (no drift). Nothing was built or run. Every claim not read directly in code is marked INFERRED. "In L_R T" = the single-split-per-ML, bridge-pair-only flat T. Vocabulary of DESIGN-N3: class (X/Y variable), unit difference (§1 formula), occupancy element and chain (§7), owner set, reserved element (§8).

What changed structurally. (a) Every non-bridge piece, bridge head, free X run interior, Y run interior tile, diagonal raster tile and prio row is an element on its current row; consecutive elements get gap 1, or 2 where tracks touch across a facing X-axis edge (NE/SW), or the flat gap (<= 2) on one flat row. So two unrelated tracks can no longer share or horizontally touch a tile after the Y snap. (b) Rules 4-6 in AddPathDiffs turn the signal-slot, waiting-room and bay rules into unit differences, with `slot_is_fixed` turning "a free link lies between slot and merge" into a template failure (refusal, not a silent gap). (c) The prio row is a reserved element of p + 1 tiles. (d) Free diagonals are variables with their own rasters in the chain and in T4's late crossings.

### 2. Gaps that are reachable in the T today (most likely on terrain first)

1. Vertical edge contact (Connectivity unexpected movement, Deadlock block join, Collision). A detour or a stretched diagonal puts a Y-edge-reaching tile on the row above or below another path's Y-edge-reaching tile at the same x. Constraint: the §7 chain extended along Y (OC2's "not adopted" §9 vertical order, edge-based gap): in each column, for consecutive occupancy elements a above b with a's tile reaching its +y edge and b's its -y edge, y_b - y_a >= 2 (else 1); owner-set rule as in X. Needs the Y positions, so it belongs after the Y snap, with X choices that move the column (an X-phase constraint on the X classes of Y-axis pieces).
2. Prio row vertical edge contact (Prio "touches a used track"). Constraint: the reserved element's two outer rows (row of M + 2 side, and the ML's row at the span) are part of the same vertical rule with the prio element's bits X (reaches no Y edge) so only other paths' Y-reaching pieces matter; or reserve the three-row footprint explicitly as an occupancy element with empty owners.
3. Footprint boundary contents. Constraint: for every point, piece and element (including diagonal rasters, the prio row, bridge heads) a unit difference to the box edge classes: value(edge) - value(class) >= 1 on x = size-1 and y = size-1, and the symmetric >= 1 at x = 0 (the box edge classes are the port ends already identified by AddBoxEdges).
4. Terrain facts on free diagonal interiors (terrain row). Constraint: domain restriction on the diagonal's first point's X class after the Y snap, from the raster at the known length (§6 facts; the code computes the rasters and discards them).
5. Doubled lane length after a detour or a diagonal stretch on one lane (Connectivity). Constraint: per movement, for each pair of doubled lanes and each pair of shared points p, q, the §1 unit difference of both lanes equal (two inequalities; late form when an X run lies between). Alternatively the detour search detours both lanes together (a structure rule, INFERRED not done).
6. Straight axis piece per prio block when a diagonal under the coverage stretches: a per-block keep_slot (an axis piece interior to a free run or k + 2 halves of the diagonal), as in rule 4.
7. Bridge overlap and span-entry (Geometry, Bridges): the span interior as an interval occupancy element (heads' tiles and the span tiles between) with owner set of the bridge's own tracks, plus "only a Y-axis (crossing) piece under an X bridge".

### 3. LL_RR-only gaps (not reachable with one split per ML, no doubled lanes beyond the bridge pair)

- Order: adjacent ML splits (>= 2 pieces): dir*(x_s2 - x_s1) >= 2 over consecutive ML splits.
- Bridge overlap on one row/column (two bridges of one axis on one line): far head < near head difference, or the interval element of item 7 above.
- Doubled lane length where lanes differ only in a free diagonal stretch (not a detour): as item 5 of the reachable list.
- Prio coverage with a lane merge or a second bridge head in the coverage (units(M) - units(head) >= 2p).
- Deadlock block joins through a shared piece of two paths (network-wide blocks): path-local rule 4 does not see them; needs the checker's block graph as constraints (INFERRED).
- `joiner_rows == 0` prios (joiner on the merge's own row, no reserved row): a prio element with side chosen by the free side; INFERRED not in the T.
