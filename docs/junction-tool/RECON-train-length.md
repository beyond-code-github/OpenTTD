## 5. Conclusions
- The engineer's expectation does not hold: only g1/g2 (and h, even_prio, prio_tiles, the S-bend sizes) scale; seven placement spots do not (A to G). The test spec (tl 4, cl 5) passes because 3cl is capped at 2tl-1, dist 2 equals the minimum there, and the room 2dy-1 = 13 >= 8; at tl 5+, or off v160, each fails.
- Two causes cover almost everything: (1) the constant `dist = 2` between chain elements and the pin of the in-edge S-bend onto its node (B, C, E, F: 4 of 7); (2) the pair floors cover only the nearest pairs (c0,c2), (c1,c2), not (c1,c3), (c0,c3) (A, D). G is a missing 2tl term.
- Not run: other topologies than T and LL_RR; speeds between; the lane split/merge floors at tl > 8; tl > 8.
