## Rules of the road (from today)
- An infeasible layer is an upstream problem: confirm WHICH model was proved infeasible (not a timeout, not a stage fed an illegal writing), then pin a unit test and fix upstream (memory: infeasible-upstream-feedback).
- The suite is unit tests: every finding lands with a two-sided pinned test; exactness asserted in the gate (memory: suite-assertions-feedback).
- Gate: intermittent SIGSEGV/SIGILL under parallel load in heavy tests (X1/K1/BR1 exact, joint recording, components T2); all pass alone and under ASan: CPU instability. Rerun the failing test alone; report.
