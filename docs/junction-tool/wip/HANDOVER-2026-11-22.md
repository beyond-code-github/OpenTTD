## What was settled today
1. **The base rules match V.** With V's 324 pins, the base LP has slack 0 at windows 8 and 4. Fixes along the way:
   - D414: a structure's crosser may cross at its piece end, i.e. bend at the crossing.
   - D415: the deck gap is 1 half tile between an axis and a diagonal crosser, and stays 2 between parallel ones.
   - Extractor: a crossing on a portal tile belongs to the other tunnel (ad1dd2e94f); port `@` positions (968bc1072f).
   - The separator lookup handles crossings held by two tunnels (223c1c5ed0).
   - Window 2 still has small mismatches: tunnel end runs (V tunnels through slopes, no ramp tile) and node rows.
2. **Keep-apart is pairwise separation, not convex cells** (the K-E5 conclusion).
   - On V's real geometry (vref, built from lengths.txt), pairwise needs slack 97-108 and cells 1884-2094, however their dummies land.
   - Convex cells are exact only when no flex step is used.
3. **Pair directions are the hard core, and they're solved in practice by reading them from earlier drawings.** D418 amends D381 for this.
   - Recipe:
     - seed = the cell-mode elastic drawing;
     - rounds = separation-only elastic LP with `elastic_tidy` 0.001, re-picking `sides_from` until slack reaches 0 (2 rounds);
     - then one integer solve.
   - Variant A (V's steps as bends) without pins: **whole 197 drawn, integer solve in 6.4 s** (cdca61376f).
   - Ruled out along the way:
     - D416 hybrid (flex groups touch nearly every wall);
     - D417 walk rule (unsafe);
     - HiGHS 1.16 lazy constraints (a placeholder only);
     - CP-SAT (no solution in 60 s; Apache-2.0 is not GPLv2-compatible anyway).

## The open problem: integrality with optional flex
- **Flex C** (flex on the end pieces of multi-piece stretches; needed for V's steps, flag `end_pieces`), unpinned:
  - LP rounds reach slack 0 in 2 rounds (28.5k pairs, box 83x109, length 2076).
  - The integer final is infeasible or undecided.
- **Ruled out as causes:**
  - freeing tight pairs (thousands are tight, because tidy drawings pack everything);
  - padding the rounds (plateaus at 44);
  - LP micro-steps (only 9 of 654 inserts are below 0.5; holding them at 0 makes it worse).
- **What remains:** 66 fractional flex lengths (1.5, 2.5, ...). Whole-tile axis pieces and half-tile parity chained along diagonals: genuine lattice coupling.
- This meets the roadmap's exit condition for the parked grid layer (§11, D334) and grid parity (D382).

## Lessons from today (process)
- An elastic drawing is not V's geometry: between pins it is arbitrary unless tidy. Only lengths.txt is the real answer key.
- Seed direction rounds from a separated drawing, and give elastic runs a length tie-break, or the sides read from them are noise.
- Brief probes for the single case that answers the question, with tight time caps.
