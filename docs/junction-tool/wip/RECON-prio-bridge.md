## 2. Constraints stated (F)
- Standard: gap between the connections becomes longer than the train, so a train on the span sits on no joined block and the prio fails: unusable for TL < 4 (Priorities.html h2 4; RULES-coop R25, R31 at l.52, 58).
- Presignal: low-TL friendly (Priorities.html h2 5); the span is one block member (signal.cpp:382-395; tools/sigsim.py:3), so one join on plain tiles either side of the head puts that block in the chain.
- Which signals are pre/exit/combo, and whether the row must bridge: image-illegible; the text implies only that presignal needs no third bridge, i.e. the row need not bridge (I).
- R31/R40/R51: a prio on a doubled/bridged line risks evil mode; best remedy is not bridging the prioritised line (RULES-coop l.58, 72, 93). D347 accepts the pattern anyway.

## 3. Our case (joiner crossing an ML lane inside coverage, the lane being a doubled bridge)
- No coop variant shows a doubled ML bridge inside coverage (above). The closest is Standard prio over a bridge (stack of parallel bridges: the doubled ML pair + the row's bridge) or the presignal variant per copy.
- I (requirements): (a) the ML lane's split before and rejoin after the copies are switches on the ML lane: they must lie outside coverage + one segment, or D108 is amended for the bridge case only (D347 says so); (b) the span (head to head) is a block member, so it must be shorter than the shortest train, or both copies need a join in the same block on a plain tile before the near head and after the far head (R25 gap rule); (c) the joiner j-1 crosses lane j beside, not on, the span: the row is gapped there (D342 allows a row leaving the ML between joins); (d) PROBE-prio-stagger.md: this is the lever that un-chains T3's three coverages.

## 4. Our checker today (junction_rules.cpp @ 0958786f36)
- CheckPrio clause 1 (join shape, 1346-1385): joins only on non-bridge pieces: `piece_index_of` skips on_bridge (1340); a bridge-head tile is not a join. The presignal variant's joins before/after the heads pass (plain axis pieces with a row tile beside). The row may leave the ML between joins (comment 1346): so a gapped row is accepted.
- Clause 4 (coverage, 1423-1450): walks coverage by units and needs exactly one join per ML block within TL (1447 "ML block within the train length needs exactly one join"). BlockOfPiece maps a span tile to the block of its start head (1192-1199). A span block with no plain axis tile is excused (`has_tile && !joinable` at 1440-1445 ignores on_bridge pieces, so a bridge-only block with no joinable tile does not need a join: I, this skips rather than judges it; the head tile hold the bridge, not an axis-only pair). G9b is the real judge.
- Clause 4b, G9c (1455-1475): rejects ANY switch of the ML lane in the 2P units before the merge (SwitchEventsOf, 433): the doubled bridge's split/rejoin is such an event: "split inside prio coverage" is the clause that rejects our case; it also fires on the standard (stacked) variant where the lane is doubled. No bridge exemption exists.
- Clause 5 (signals, 1478-1510): prio signals must lie on the row between consecutive joins (1498 `between`), exactly one per segment, farthest Exit, others Combo (1496-1497); faces_upstream looks at non-bridge ML pieces only (1487). A signal on the row beside a span passes; the coop's rows have no signal on the bridge anyway (signals only on plain tiles, rail_cmd.cpp:1077; RESEARCH-prio-freedom.md:7).
- CheckPrioReach G9b (1688-1780): covered pieces = coverage walk plus the piece beyond a signalled end (1740-1751); a span tile is tested as its start head (1758-1766); SettleSignals treats a bridge as a wormhole (1557-1565, 1604). So it already accepts the bridge as ONE block member: the presignal variant passes G9b if a one-tile train on either head or span settles the joiner's Entry red, i.e. the heads' block is joined on plain tiles (I). It accepts the standard variant on the same condition.
- Net (I): to allow D347 only G9c (1455-1475) needs an exemption (copy split/rejoin of a doubled bridge, the lane's switches counted but G9b judging); clauses 1, 4, 5 and G9b need no change for the presignal-style rhombus.
