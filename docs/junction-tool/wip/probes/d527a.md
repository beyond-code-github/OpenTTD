Paragraph: 16 of the 32 moves are refused with rip. 3 of those 16 have at least one unlocker: node 3 north (one: node 4 to (19,22)), node 5 west (one: node 4 to (19,22)) and node 6 south (three: the node at (16,3) to (18,1), the node at (17,18) to (19,20) or (17,20)). The other 13, among them node 0 north (the test case), have none among the neighbours and the nodes under the soft routes, so a single-node move does not unlock them; they need a cascade (D508) or are legitimately blocked. Every call finished complete within its 5 s budget; one call is about 8 node moves per candidate at about 0.07 to 0.1 s each, so the slowest (node 4 west, 40 moves, 5 candidates) took 5.03 s, just past the budget because the clock is checked before each move.
