## 1. Shape's model in ten lines

1. A track has events (its crossings, in travel order). A stretch is the track between two consecutive events, or from its start to the first event, or from the last event to its end (`H[t]` has events+2 points, PC:1411-1414; `FreeOnePieceStretch` doc, PC:940-947).
2. Shape's one MILP decides a lifted integer heading (eighths, -24..32) at every point of every track (PC:1413). It decides nothing else: no lengths, no positions, no sideways moves (SPEC-shape §2, D360, D362).
3. A stretch turns monotonically: pieces walk one eighth at a time from the start heading to the end heading (PC:1700-1706; H1; SPEC-shape §2 item 1). So a stretch with turn n has n+1 pieces, a straight one has 1 piece. A turn and a turn back is a sideways move, so by SPEC §2 it is "never Shape's".
4. Rows: ports fix end headings (PC:1417-1422); topology nodes: the straight leg keeps h, every other leg is exactly one eighth off toward its side (d = 1, D400 node rule, D404; PC:1456); crossings: `H_crosser - H_sep = s*a + 8q`, a in 1..3, separator on an axis (PC:1482-1491, H4/H5); mirrored crossings share the original's angle and piece (PC:1495-1503); capacity per span with H11 runs (D376, D396; PC:1505-1545); spines H14 (D388 a, D403); beside tracks copy their parent chain (H8); each face winds once (H7); no U-turn within 3 eighths (PC:1634).
5. Objective: total turning of the Line tracks, 1000 per eighth, nothing else (PC:1671-1678). The crossing angle variable has cost 0 (`var(1, 3, 0, true)`, PC:1482), so there is no angle preference (SPEC-shape §2.1 "Cost").
6. A crossing's angle is whatever the two headings at the event give. Headings are free between the node rule, the port headings and the monotone-turn rule, so among equal-turning solutions the solver's tie order decides where on a stretch the turn happens and so the angle.
7. After the solve, `PlacePlanarShapeFlex` (PC:973-1030) inserts flex pairs into `pieces` (D397, D402, D420). A flex pair is the two pieces (h-1, h) or (h+1, h) after piece p; Slots sizes it >= 0, 0 is no step (header `planar_shape_layer.h:154-162`).
8. Where: on every free stretch (`FreeOnePieceStretch`, PC:948-971: Line track; not both crossings of one structure or its copy span; not two crossings with tracks of one channel (PC:963); track not in a beside track's parent chain). One-piece stretch: the pairs go after its single piece. Flex C (D420, default `end_pieces = true`): a multi-piece stretch gets them after its first and after its last piece only (PC:993, 1025-1026). Each side only where every route through it stays within 3 eighths (PC:996-1015).
9. Why one pair per side per place: D383 defines a flex point as one pair of inserts (h+1, odd diagonal part, h-1) of >= 0 lanes; D397 gives "the bend pairs h -> h-1 -> h and h -> h+1 -> h", each sized >= 0. No decision argues for exactly one: it is the definition of the mark, kept small because every flex mark is a bend in H12 cells and a wall in Slots' rows (D413: flex on every piece gave 660 marks and cell slack 233 -> 1060).
10. What a step is: D395 (RETIRED by D431) had Shape place a step by choice; D397 replaced that by rule: no step is Shape's decision, each flex pair is a potential step and Slots' footprint decides whether its size is 0. So Shape never "emits" a step; it offers up to two pairs per side per stretch (one per flex position) and the V steps are matched against them (option-A probe, planar_draw.cpp:2081-2180).

## 3. TS2

Topology (`planar_domain_encoding.cpp:2895`): W in E, N out N, E out E; AB1..AB3 split into A1..A3 and B1..B3; A2, A3 cross B1 (tunnel T1); A3 crosses B2 (tunnel T2). From `TS2_DUMP`: A1, A2, A3 pieces `1 0` (leg at 1, then 0), AB and B pieces `2`.

- Which constraint chose 90 degrees at A3 x B2: none. The crossing row (PC:1490) lets a = 1, 2 or 3; the capacity rows only count runs against the tunnel limit (PC:1505-1545), not an angle preference; the objective counts turning only, and A3 turns exactly once (leg heading 1 at the node, port heading 0) wherever that turn sits. Crossing at heading 1 (a = 1) and turning after the last crossing costs the same as crossing at heading 0 (a = 2). SPEC-shape §2.1 states "no angle preference among equal turning" on purpose. So the solver's tie order produced `1 0` with the crossing on the 0 piece. I did not re-solve with a = 1 forced to show it feasible; the rows allow it by reading them (heading 1 crossing B2 at heading 2 from the right is a = 1).
- What Shape knows about port spacing: nothing. No row in `ShapePlanarStructureVariant` reads `side_gap`, a port position or a lane count; `side_gap` is on the variant (`planar_structure.h:66`) with the note "Slots reads it, never the spec" (D377 Q3), and `PlanarTopology::at` (`planar_topology.h:45`) is read only by the drawing program. The default spacing is one lane, the gap needed is 4 tiles per lane at W and E (`wip/probes/domain/ts2-diag.md`, solves 37, 38, 40; 1, 2, 3 tiles infeasible).
- The conflict itself (ts2-diag.md, 9 rows): A3's diagonal leg rise plus the clearance of its turn to north has to fit between lane 3 and lane 2; a crossing taken at 45 degrees from the same node does not have the bend between the lanes. Shape cannot see that without spacing.

## 4. Decisions that would have to change

For L3, L85, L72 (multiplicity):
- D383 / D397 / D402 / D420: the flex mark is one pair per side per place. Candidates: two pairs per side at a place, or a second place on a one-piece stretch. D413's measurements bear on the cost: every extra mark is a cell wall and a Slots row (660 marks on 197 with flex on every piece; D413 was retired by D431, flex C stays on the suite's retest list, D431 (2)). Measured need on 197 is 3 tracks of 34 steps entries (L3, L85, L72), so a narrower rule (a second pair only where Shape's heading list is a straight or end-piece stretch) is the smaller change; not tried.
- SPEC-shape §2 item 1 states a turn and turn back "is never Shape's"; D397 already moved it to rule, so only the count changes.

For L38, L44, L77 (nothing, but the evidence trail):
- D402's "one flex pair per free stretch" was why they looked unfit; D420 replaced it. The domain-36 finding should be re-read against D420: it names these as not carried, which holds only for `end_pieces = false`. D413's trial (flex on every piece) is not needed for them; D420's end pieces cover them.

For L89:
- D383's lattice rigidity and D397's channel clause (`FreeOnePieceStretch`, PC:963). V steps once between two crossings of two structures on one channel (T37, T39). A narrower clause (exempt a stretch whose two crossings are of two different structures even in one channel) would carry it; whether Slots can size such a step between two tunnel decks is not shown here (G1w's argument was about a crosser climbing within one bundle's lattice, not between two tunnels).

For TS2:
- No decision binds Shape here; to rule out the 90 degree choice at tight spacing Shape would have to read the gaps: amends D377 Q3 ("Slots reads it") and SPEC-shape §1 ("nothing else. No lengths or positions exist yet") and SPEC-shape §2.1 "no angle preference". The alternative, keeping Shape blind and widening the TS2 gap in the input, is what the domain suite did (4 tiles per lane). The 90 versus 45 choice is a tie in Shape's own objective, so a tie-break toward 45 where lanes are tight is the smallest Shape-side change; not designed or tested.
